## What the trades show (anecdote, not evidence — every bucket is under 10)

1. **Signal exits came too late.** The 10 trades closed on a signal reversal
   lost a combined −$444 and only 2 of them made money. By the time price
   broke 3% below SMA10, most calls had already lost 40–92% of premium.
2. **The 7-day time stop closed the winners.** The 6 time-stop exits made
   +$786, including the three biggest wins (WMT +272%, GME +144%, NKE put
   +88%). Those trades were still working when the rule closed them, so the
   time stop may also be capping upside.
3. **Losers ride close to zero.** Half the losses were worse than −60%, and
   XLF lost 99% (sold at $0.01). Nothing in the rules cuts a loser on the
   option's own price.
4. **Puts 4 of 5, calls 3 of 12.** Interesting, but 5 puts is nothing, and
   the market moved down in Nov while most calls ran in Aug–Oct.
5. **Entries matched the rules.** 15 of 17 reconstructed entries reproduce
   the SMA/RSI conditions; the two that don't (GME Oct 19 call, INTC Nov 12
   put) are within the noise of the reconstruction. Estimated deltas sat in
   0.29–0.59, mostly 0.45–0.55. No trade had earnings before expiry.

## What this sample can and can't support

- **Can:** describe what happened, flag where to look (exit timing, a stop on
  the option's own price, the time stop on winners).
- **Can't:** justify any rule change. Every comparison above is 1–12 trades,
  one outlier decides the total, and 16 of 17 closed trades ran under an
  older rules version (before the Nov 18 scaling change).
- **Rough bar for real conclusions:** about 50 closed trades overall, with at
  least 10 on each side of any comparison, and a result that survives
  removing the best trade. At the pace so far (about 4 closes a month,
  likely more now that the limits scale with the account) that's several
  months out.

## Data caveats

- SMA10/SMA30/RSI14 at entry and exit are **reconstructed** from Robinhood
  daily bars, using the 30-minute bar at each fill as the live price. The
  agent's own live values may differ slightly. From now on, runs record the
  live values (`signal_source=live`).
- Delta and IV are **Black-Scholes estimates** (r = 4%, no dividends) backed
  out of the fill price; they are not Robinhood's greeks.
- Exit reasons are **inferred**. `signal_exit_unconfirmed` means the agent
  closed before 7 DTE but the reconstructed indicators don't show the exit
  trigger at that moment.
- NFLX 79C (Aug 26) was closed manually in the app, not by the agent.
- 16 trades ran under rules version `7cbbc08`, which capped a contract at
  $260 (later $500, now 15% of account value).
